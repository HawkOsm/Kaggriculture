## 2. Competition-Specific Rules

### 2.1 Team Limits
- Maximum team size: **5**.
- Team mergers are allowed by the team leader, provided the combined team's total submission count is within the max allowed as of the Team Merger Deadline (submissions/day × days competition has run).

### 2.2 Submission Limits
- Maximum **5 submissions per day**.
- Up to **2 Final Submissions** may be selected for judging.

### 2.3 Competition Timeline
See the Overview → Timeline page for authoritative dates. As fetched:
- **July 29, 2026** — Start Date
- **September 23, 2026** — Entry Deadline (must accept rules by this date)
- **September 23, 2026** — Team Merger Deadline
- **September 30, 2026** — Final Submission Deadline
- **October 1 – ~October 15, 2026** — Games continue running until leaderboard convergence; leaderboard is then final.
- All deadlines are 11:59 PM UTC on the stated day unless noted otherwise. Sponsor may update the timeline.

### 2.4 Competition Data
- **Data Access and Use:** You may access/use the Competition Data for any purpose (commercial or non-commercial), including participating in the competition, forums, academic research, and education.
- **Data Security:** You must use reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, and must not transmit/publish/redistribute the Competition Data to non-participants. Report any unauthorized access to Kaggle immediately.

### 2.5 Winner License
- Winning submissions and their source code must be licensed under **CC-BY 4.0** (an OSI-approved open license that never limits commercial use).
- Generally commercially available third-party software you used does not need to be separately licensed if procurable by the Sponsor without undue expense.
- Input data / pretrained models under an incompatible license do not need to be open-sourced.
- Winners may need to provide a detailed methodology writeup (architecture, preprocessing, training details, hyperparameters, etc.) with a link to a reproducible code repository, and may be asked to discuss results on a recorded call.

### 2.6 External Data and Tools
- External Data may be used to develop/test submissions, provided it's publicly available and equally accessible to all participants at no cost, or satisfies the "Reasonableness Standard" (cost/accessibility assessed by the Host).
- Automated Machine Learning Tools (AMLT, e.g. Google AutoML, H2O Driverless AI) may be used provided you hold an appropriate license.

### 2.7 Eligibility
- Employees/interns/contractors/officers/directors of Competition Entities (Sponsor, Kaggle, affiliates) may participate but are **not eligible to win prizes**, subject to their employer's internal policies.

### 2.8 Winner's Obligations
- Provide a detailed description of how the winning submission was generated, posted in the Competition forums, sufficient for reproduction (architecture, preprocessing, loss function, training details, hyperparameters, etc.), plus a link to a reproducible code repository.
- Grant the Sponsor the license stated above and warrant you have the right to grant it.
- Sign and return all required prize acceptance documents (eligibility certifications, licenses/releases, U.S. tax forms such as W-9/W-8BEN).

### 2.9 Governing Law
- California law governs; exclusive jurisdiction is Federal/State courts of Santa Clara County, California, USA.

### 2.10 Scoring and Leaderboard
- Submissions are scored based on performance across episodes, aggregated into a Leaderboard position.
- **There is no Private Leaderboard in Simulation competitions.**

### 2.11 Environments & Public Availability
- Uses Kaggle Environments. Episode replays (including your submission's actions) may be publicly available/downloadable.

### 2.12 No Ingress or Egress
- During episode evaluation, your submission **may not pull in or use information external to the Submission and Environment, and may not send information out.**

## 3. General Competition Rules (Binding Agreement)

### 3.1 Eligibility
- Must be a registered Kaggle.com account holder, at least 18 (or age of majority in your jurisdiction).
- Must **not** be a resident of Crimea, so-called DNR/LNR, Cuba, Iran, or North Korea, and not subject to U.S. export controls/sanctions.
- Open to residents worldwide subject to the above exclusions and local law; Sponsor may substitute alternative prizes where legally required.
- Entering on behalf of a company/institution/employer binds that entity too; you warrant you're acting with their knowledge/consent.

### 3.2 Sponsor and Hosting Platform
- Hosted by Kaggle Inc. on behalf of the Sponsor (Google LLC); Kaggle is an independent contractor, not a party to your agreement with the Sponsor. Kaggle Terms of Service also apply (www.kaggle.com/terms).

### 3.3 Competition Period
- Runs from Start Date to Final Submission Deadline. Sponsor may introduce additional deadlines; check the Competition Website regularly. You are responsible for converting to your local time zone.

### 3.4 Competition Entry
- No purchase necessary. Register on the Competition Website before the Entry Deadline and follow submission Requirements.
- Submissions may not use hand labeling or human prediction of validation/test data (except where a Hackathon expressly allows it).
- Void submissions: illegible, incomplete, damaged, altered, counterfeit, fraudulent, or late.

### 3.5 Individuals and Teams
- One unique Kaggle account per person; multi-accounting causes disqualification.
- Team formation/merging permitted per Competition Website; each member needs a separate Kaggle account and must confirm membership.
- **No private sharing outside teams** — code/data sharing outside your team is not permitted; it's fine to share on public forums for all competitors.

### 3.6 Submission Code Requirements
- **Private Code Sharing** of Competition Code is prohibited during the Competition Period (including between separate teams, unless merged) — breach may cause disqualification.
- **Public Code Sharing** is allowed via Kaggle.com's competition forum/notebooks; publicly shared code is deemed licensed under an OSI-approved open license.
- Any open-source code used in your submission must itself be OSI-licensed with no commercial-use restrictions.

### 3.7 Determining Winners
- Ranked by the evaluation metric on the Public Leaderboard during the competition; final winners determined by the Private Leaderboard where applicable (n/a here — simulation comp has no private leaderboard, see §2.10).
- Ties go to whichever submission was entered first; disqualified winners are replaced by the next-highest scorer.

### 3.8 Notification of Winners & Disqualification
- Winners notified by email; must respond within 1 week or forfeit. Sponsor may disqualify for cheating, deception, unfair practices, or harassment.

### 3.9 Prizes
- Subject to eligibility/compliance verification. Non-compliant submissions may be disqualified or given 1 week to remediate (e.g., license conflicts).
- Prize acceptance documents due within 2 weeks of request, or forfeiture. Prizes paid ~30 days after documents received. No prize transfer/assignment.
- Team prizes split evenly unless the team unanimously agrees otherwise before payout.

### 3.10 Taxes
- Winners are solely responsible for all taxes on prizes; required tax documentation must be submitted. U.S. residents receive IRS Form 1099.

### 3.12 Publicity
- Sponsor/Kaggle/affiliates may use your name and likeness for advertising/promotion without extra compensation, unless prohibited by law.

### 3.13 Privacy
- Kaggle and Sponsor may collect/store/share/use your personal information (name, address, phone, email) for competition administration, per Kaggle's Privacy Policy (www.kaggle.com/privacy). Your data may be transferred to the Sponsor's country.

### 3.14 Warranty, Indemnity and Release
- You warrant your submission is your own original work, doesn't infringe third-party rights, and doesn't violate applicable law.
- You indemnify the Competition Entities against claims arising from your submission, misrepresentations, non-compliance, or misuse of a prize.

### 3.15 Internet
- Competition Entities aren't responsible for platform malfunctions or lost/corrupted submissions due to technical failures.

### 3.16 Right to Cancel, Modify or Disqualify
- Sponsor may cancel/modify/suspend the competition for integrity-threatening events (fraud, tampering, technical failure, etc.), and may disqualify tamperers; legal action may be pursued for deliberate interference.

### 3.17 Not an Offer of Employment
- Nothing in these Rules or your submission constitutes an employment offer or fiduciary relationship with the Sponsor.

### 3.18 Definitions (selected)
- **Competition Data** — data/datasets (incl. any prototype/executable code) available from the Competition Website; contains private and public test sets, allocation undisclosed to participants.
- **Entry** — joining/signing up/accepting the rules of a competition.
- **Final Submission** — the submission(s) selected (by user or default) for final leaderboard placement.
- **Participant** — an individual who enters and makes a submission.
- **Private Leaderboard** — ranking against the private test set; determines final standing (n/a to this simulation competition, see §2.10).
- **Public Leaderboard** — ranking against the public/representative test data, visible throughout.
- **Sponsor** — hosts the competition (data, winner determination, rule enforcement).
- **Submission** — anything a Participant provides to be evaluated (model, notebook, prediction file, or other Sponsor-determined format).
- **Team** — one or more Participants who have officially merged on the competition platform.
